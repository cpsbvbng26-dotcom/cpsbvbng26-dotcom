Ancestral record

Ancestors for whom a public record survives. Identifiers are given; the contents of those records have not been checked. This page says nothing about the author’s work.

as of 11 September 2026

What this page is

This page says nothing about the author's work. What an ancestor did is not evidence for the correctness of anything the author has published. What is listed here is one thing only: that a public record survives.

There are two reasons for putting it up. First. The source note on the naval gazette was written out of kinship, and the paper itself does not say so. Handling a source without disclosing how one came to it is weak scholarly practice. Second. What survives in the public record can be consulted by anyone. What can be consulted is written down.

Grounds of different strength are not mixed. What stands on a document, what stands on testimony, and what has not been cross-checked are kept apart in the list. The same division the register uses.

No language of lineage or distinction is used. What this page treats is whether a record survives, nothing else. The words it does not use are held by a machine (check_site.js).

Declining to assert identity is the heaviest reservation on this page. That a name matches and that a person is the same are two different things. As long as they have not been cross-checked, they are not written as joined.

Nemoto Kaichiro — great-great-grandfather

A certified copy of the family register carries the name Nemoto Kaichiro. He is the author's great-great-grandfather. That much stands on a document.

What the grandfather said is that Nemoto Kaichiro was an assembly member. The author gives the office as the Yokokawa village assembly. What the grandfather said reached only as far as "he was an assembly member".

The two printed books were not cited by the grandfather. The identifiers came from the author. Joining the two is not the grandfather's testimony. Whether those books carry the name has not been checked here.

These two are not joined. Whether the person in the books and the person in the register are the same, this page does not assert. Dates of birth and death, term of office, and any other deeds are not written either.

Otani Tsune — great-grandfather

The author's great-grandfather was a person named Otani Tsune. This was held in common within the family, and as a small child the author lit incense for him at the household altar. It has not been cross-checked against a document.

What the source document supports is this. On 11 September 1943, Lieutenant Otani Tsune received the Order of the Sacred Treasure, Fourth Class. The provisional entry number is 2603-25. The source is held at the Japan Center for Asian Historical Records (JACAR), Ref. C12070491000, a special issue of the naval gazette (internal circulation).

These two are not joined. Whether the person in the gazette and the great-grandfather are the same, this page does not assert.

There is a source note on the gazette entry. It gives a transcription, and separates what can be said from what cannot.

A Naval Gazette Entry for Lieutenant Otani Tsune (10.5281/zenodo.22055709)

The reading of the name is not settled. The note's own abstract states It does not establish the reading of his name. That the title nevertheless supplies a reading is recorded in its errata (E5).

No image of the source document is posted. Reproduction requires clearance from the Center for Military History, National Institute for Defense Studies, Ministry of Defense.

That medium cannot be opened from the working environment, so its contents have not been checked.

The problem is the grade of the evidence

The source note states that the gazette alone cannot settle final rank, ship, or posting. If the same author settles those in a different medium, two standards are in use.

SubjectWhich is authoritative
Rank, decoration and entry number as of 11 September 1943The source note (written against the source document)
Final rank, ship, postingNothing is authoritative
RelationshipThis page (grounds separated by grade)

This page does not write what the gazette cannot settle. No final rank, no ship, no posting. That they are absent is held by a machine (check_site.js).

There are only two ways to reconcile them. Attach a further source that supports the assertion in the other medium, or drop the assertion. This page will not be brought down to the weaker grade. The handling is set out in canonical-sources.md, and that it remains unfinished is recorded as ST-004 in the register.

Requests for correction or removal

Requests to correct or remove anything on this page are accepted. Via the issues of this repository. On a request, it is checked and then corrected or taken down. What was corrected is kept in the register.

The same address accepts requests from anyone who would rather not appear in a public place. No reason need be given.

What has not been checked

  • The contents of the sources. Neither the National Diet Library Digital Collections nor JACAR can be opened from the working environment. The identifiers were supplied by the author
  • The relevant page. Which page carries which entry has not been seen
  • Whether the books carry the name at all. That the two printed books carry this name has itself not been checked. The grandfather's testimony does not extend to the books — what was heard reached only as far as his having been an assembly member, and it was not the grandfather who cited them
  • The register against the printed books. The name is confirmed present in the certified copy of the family register, but whether the entries in the two books refer to the same person has not been cross-checked here
  • The possibility of a namesake. The author states that it was ruled out. The procedure by which it was ruled out is not written on this page
  • Otani Tsune's relationship. Held in common within the family, not cross-checked against a document

How to overturn this. Show that one of the sources above does not carry the person named, and that row falls. Showing that the person in the printed books is someone else does the same.
