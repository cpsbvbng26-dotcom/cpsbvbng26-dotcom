## Taking the principle of ki as a subject of research

### Position

I trained in Shinshin Toitsu Aikido (心身統一合氣道) for 7 years.
The organisation I trained under is the Shinshin Toitsu Aikido Kai (心身統一合氣道会).
I am a party to the subject: for 4 years I studied under a teacher (師範) of the school, learning the principle of ki, the unified body (統一体), the empty-hand techniques (体技), and the sword, staff and knife of aikido (合氣剣・合氣杖・合氣短刀).
In my time at fifth kyu (五級), I was also among those whom the teacher rated highly.
This is my own account, not a verified fact.
Having been rated highly inside the school is itself a source of bias.

The same experience is written on other pages.
[The page on the solitary school](../notes/solitary-school.html) says 7 years in all, in Tochigi, where the school has its home.
The section 「武道」 of [the CV](../cv.html) lists fifth kyu of Shinshin Toitsu Aikido and the first grade (初級) of Shinshin Toitsu Dō (心身統一道), as self-reported.
Fifth kyu was my last promotion in Shinshin Toitsu Aikido.
The first grade of Shinshin Toitsu Dō is the grade that recognises being able to put the principle of ki into practice in daily life.

The teacher's name is not written here.
Nor is anything that would identify the teacher.
This is because rule 10 limits what is written about living persons.

The name of the organisation is written to disclose the history of my affiliation.
This record has not been approved by the organisation.
Its name is not used to support any claim made here.
The page on the solitary school does not name the organisation, because it does not use the history of others to support its own name.
This record discloses rather than claims a name, so it names the organisation.
The author decided on 2026-11-20 that the name of the organisation may be written.

### Why the position cannot be neutral

If I research the principle of ki from this position, the conclusion cannot be neutral, whichever way it falls.
A conclusion that affirms it would support my own background and the authority of my teacher and the school.
A conclusion that denies it would be subject to deference to, or pressure from, the relationship between teacher and student.
I therefore cannot treat this subject as an "outside researcher".

### Conditions under which it could be handled

If it is handled at all, it is only as a first-person record.
My position and the relationship between teacher and student are disclosed.
Experience is kept apart from verification.
It is not handled in any form that lacks one of these.

### Not added to the self-taught series

This record does not start a new research topic.
The subject is not added to the self-taught series.
Under rule 15, the self-taught work is what was done up to the first undergraduate year.
If the subject is handled at all, it goes by the routes that rule 15 names.
These are routes such as JOSS, diamond open access venues, conference presentations and joint research.

### Not handled on Sophaxis

Sophaxis, which I operate, does not handle this subject.
Handling research that touches the operator's own background on the operator's own platform is too strong a conflict of interest.
On the side of Sophaxis, this is written under "Conflicts of interest" in [the Moderation Policy](https://sophaxis.vercel.app/policies/moderation.html#conflicts-of-interest).
From 2026-12-15, Sophaxis lists no work whose subject is the principle of ki, whoever submits it.
The change narrows what Sophaxis accepts, so it is announced the period ahead that the Terms of Use of Sophaxis set.

### This is not a reason not to research it

This is not a record of a reason not to research the subject; it is a record of the conditions under which it could be handled.

## Dormant conflicts of interest (those that wake when the subject comes close)

The author decided on 2026-11-20 to record this section.

Claude wrote it.
What is set out here is weak now.
It becomes stronger only when the subject comes close.

### A supporter registered with the headquarters of the Democratic Party for the People (国民民主党の本部登録ファン)

I am a supporter registered with the headquarters of the Democratic Party for the People (国民民主党).
The author chose to publish the name of the party.
A disclosure means nothing if readers cannot tell which party it is.
A political view can be a creed (「信条」) under Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information.
Here the author publishes it themself.

It is weak now.
I hold no office, no money passes either way, and I have posted almost nothing about the party.

It wakes when I write, as a researcher's analysis, about these subjects: social institutions, models of class and stratification, education policy, youth policy, and policy on digital talent.

The pull runs both ways.
A conclusion close to the party's policy can read as political endorsement in academic dress.
Criticism of other parties or of present institutions can read as a defence of the party I support.
Registration with the headquarters carries more formality than a private vote.

There is also a benefit of exposure.
If the party or its politicians spread the work, the reach of research and political support are exchanged, even with no money involved.
At that point this conflict of interest is of medium strength.

### The university where I am enrolled (an online university)

The name of the university is not written in this record.

It is weak now.
I am not on the university's staff, and I have no role in promoting it.
What I record about the university is fact: the credits earned, the requirements, and the preparation of applications.

It wakes when I evaluate that university, or the value of online degrees in general.
My own path is online study now, then OMSCS, then an online doctorate.
Each of these is an aim recorded on this site.

The pull runs both ways.
Praise can look like flattery of those who grant credits, recommendations and graduation.
Criticism may be suspected of having been softened for fear of a disadvantage.
Public praise near the time of a recommendation can look like a trade.

### When they overlap

Where the party and the university overlap, weak and weak become medium.
The case is a person who supports the party, is enrolled at an online university and means to advance by that route, writing about education policy.
A subject such as "the promotion of online education" draws in the party, the university and my own path alike.

### Conditions for handling them

When I write on these subjects, the disclosure comes first.
Facts and evaluation are kept apart.
No general evaluation of the university where I am enrolled, or of online degrees, is written as research.
