Legal handling of the design

デザインに関する法的な扱い

This page sets out how each visual part of this site was made, where it came from, its rights and licence, and the articles of law it touches. It is the operator’s own understanding, not legal advice. What applies is the Disclaimer and the licences.

About this page

This page sets out, for each visual part of this site, who made it, where it came from, what rights and licences apply, and which articles of law it touches.

The site serves the same files from the same repository at two places: cpsbvbng26-dotcom.github.io and cpsbvbng26-dotcom.vercel.app.

The parts are the typefaces, the logo and the sharing images, the page ornaments, the figure, the colours, the layout, and whether anything is loaded from outside.

What is written here is the operator’s own understanding. It is not legal advice.

This page has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

Statutes are quoted in the original Japanese, word for word, from copies fetched from the e-Gov law API. The English beside them is a summary, not a translation; no government translation is used or guessed. The source of the copies is given in the last section.

This page creates no right or obligation. What applies is the Disclaimer and this repository’s licences (LICENSE and LICENSE-CODE). This page is a translation of the Japanese page, which prevails where they differ.

Typefaces, and the letters on the share cards

The typefaces named in the page CSS are Hiragino Sans, Hiragino Kaku Gothic ProN, Hiragino Mincho ProN, Noto Sans JP, Noto Serif JP, Yu Gothic, Yu Mincho, YuMincho, Meiryo, Georgia, Times New Roman, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Consolas. The generic names system-ui, sans-serif, serif, ui-monospace, monospace are also used, which point to the device’s defaults.

All of them only name typefaces on the reader’s device. This site serves no font file.

The letters on the share cards are drawn with the list in verification/update_cards.js ("Hiragino Sans", "Noto Sans CJK JP", "IPAGothic", "IPAPGothic", sans-serif).

The environment that drew the present cards has neither of the first two typefaces in that list.

The browser in that environment was asked which typefaces drew the letters on the cards (CSS.getPlatformFontsForNode in DevTools, 2026-11-22). Every letter was drawn in IPAPGothic (IPA P Gothic), except the sign “≥” on the two cards of the third paper of the operator series, which was drawn in Liberation Sans.

Redrawing the cards in the same environment gives every card identical to the bit.

Article 2 of the IPAPGothic licence (IPA Font License v1.0) reads as follows, quoted from the licence text of the package (fonts-ipafont-gothic) in that environment (Japanese original).

2.受領者はコンピュータにインストールされた許諾プログラムをそのまま、または改変を行ったうえで、印刷物およびデジタル・コンテンツにおいて、文字テキスト表現等として使用することができます。
3.受領者は前項の定めに従い作成した印刷物およびデジタル・コンテンツにつき、その商用・非商用の別、および放送、通信、各種記録メディアなどの媒体の形式を問わず、複製その他の利用をすることができます。

Summary, not a translation: the recipient may use the installed font program as text in printed matter and digital content, and may copy and otherwise use that printed matter and digital content, commercially or not, in any medium.

Condition 5 of the Liberation Sans licence (SIL Open Font License 1.1) reads as follows, quoted from the licence text of the package (fonts-liberation) in the same environment.

5) The Font Software, modified or unmodified, in part or in whole, must be distributed entirely under this license, and must not be distributed under any other license. The requirement for fonts to remain under this license does not apply to any document created using the Font Software.

The share cards use the typefaces as lettering inside images, within those provisions. The font files themselves are in neither the cards nor the pages.

Copyright

The Copyright Act defines a work as follows, and gives works of fine art as an example.

Copyright Act, Article 2(1)(i) (Japanese original)

一 著作物 思想又は感情を創作的に表現したものであつて、文芸、学術、美術又は音楽の範囲に属するものをいう。

Summary, not a translation: a work is a creative expression of thoughts or sentiments that falls within the literary, academic, artistic or musical domain.

Copyright Act, Article 10(1)(iv) (Japanese original)

四 絵画、版画、彫刻その他の美術の著作物

Summary, not a translation: paintings, engravings, sculptures and other works of fine art.

Whether the logo, ornaments, figure, colours or layout of this site are works of fine art is not settled by the text of the Act. This page decides neither way.

For whatever parts are works, the licences are as divided in the licence section of the README: the logo and the share-card images are CC BY 4.0 (LICENSE), and the page markup, styles and scripts are MIT (LICENSE-CODE). This page does not change that division.

The preface of the CC BY 4.0 LICENSE (not part of the licence itself) says that a licence grants only the rights the licensor holds.

Our licenses grant only permissions under copyright and certain other rights that a licensor has authority to grant.

On moral rights, the Copyright Act provides as follows. Only paragraph 1 of each article is cut out.

Copyright Act, Article 19(1) (Japanese original)

著作者は、その著作物の原作品に、又はその著作物の公衆への提供若しくは提示に際し、その実名若しくは変名を著作者名として表示し、又は著作者名を表示しないこととする権利を有する。 その著作物を原著作物とする二次的著作物の公衆への提供又は提示に際しての原著作物の著作者名の表示についても、同様とする。

Summary, not a translation: the author has the right to have their real name or a pseudonym shown as the author, or to have no name shown, and the same holds for the original author of a derivative work.

Copyright Act, Article 20(1) (Japanese original)

著作者は、その著作物及びその題号の同一性を保持する権利を有し、その意に反してこれらの変更、切除その他の改変を受けないものとする。

Summary, not a translation: the author has the right to preserve the integrity of the work and its title, against changes, cuts or other modification against the author’s will.

Section 2(b)(1) of CC BY 4.0 leaves moral rights outside the licence, and the licensor agrees not to assert them to the limited extent needed for the licensed rights to be used.

Moral rights, such as the right of integrity, are not licensed under this Public License, nor are publicity, privacy, and/or other similar personality rights; however, to the extent possible, the Licensor waives and/or agrees not to assert any such rights held by the Licensor to the limited extent necessary to allow You to exercise the Licensed Rights, but not otherwise.

The text of the MIT licence says nothing about moral rights.

The logo SVG and the code of the ornaments and the figure were written with Claude Code (Anthropic) at the operator’s request. The operator checked them and is responsible for them. Claude is not an author (see “Use of language models” in the Disclaimer).

No other person’s image, design or font file is used in any part. Only the letter shapes on the share cards belong to IPAPGothic and Liberation Sans, under the licences quoted in the previous section.

Trademarks, designs and unfair competition

This repository holds no record that the operator has registered the name or the logo as a trademark or a design. The records of the Japan Patent Office have not been checked.

The rights in registered trademarks and designs are set by the Trademark Act and the Design Act. Only the first sentence is cut out of Article 25 and Article 23, and only paragraph 1 of Article 2.

Trademark Act, Article 25 (Japanese original)

商標権者は、指定商品又は指定役務について登録商標の使用をする権利を専有する。

Summary, not a translation: the holder of a trademark right has the exclusive right to use the registered trademark for the designated goods or services.

Design Act, Article 2(1) (Japanese original)

この法律で「意匠」とは、物品（物品の部分を含む。以下同じ。）の形状、模様若しくは色彩若しくはこれらの結合（以下「形状等」という。）、建築物（建築物の部分を含む。以下同じ。）の形状等又は画像（機器の操作の用に供されるもの又は機器がその機能を発揮した結果として表示されるものに限り、画像の部分を含む。次条第二項、第三十七条第二項、第三十八条第七号及び第八号、第四十四条の三第二項第六号並びに第五十五条第二項第六号を除き、以下同じ。）であつて、視覚を通じて美感を起こさせるものをいう。

Summary, not a translation: a design is the shape, pattern or colour (or their combination) of an article or a building, or an image used to operate a device or shown as the result of its function, that creates an aesthetic impression through the eye.

Design Act, Article 23 (Japanese original)

意匠権者は、業として登録意匠及びこれに類似する意匠の実施をする権利を専有する。

Summary, not a translation: the holder of a design right has the exclusive right to work the registered design, and designs similar to it, as a business.

Whether this logo or the look of these pages is the same as or similar to someone else’s registered trademark or design has not been checked, because the registers have not been searched.

This site is free of charge and sells no goods or services. Whether that keeps it outside “as a business” is not settled by the text of the Act. This page does not decide it.

The Unfair Competition Prevention Act counts the following as unfair competition (items 1 to 3 of Article 2(1) are cut out).

Unfair Competition Prevention Act, Article 2(1)(i) (Japanese original)

一 他人の商品等表示（人の業務に係る氏名、商号、商標、標章、商品の容器若しくは包装その他の商品又は営業を表示するものをいう。以下同じ。）として需要者の間に広く認識されているものと同一若しくは類似の商品等表示を使用し、又はその商品等表示を使用した商品を譲渡し、引き渡し、譲渡若しくは引渡しのために展示し、輸出し、輸入し、若しくは電気通信回線を通じて提供して、他人の商品又は営業と混同を生じさせる行為

Summary, not a translation: using an indication that is the same as or similar to another person’s indication of goods or business that is well known among consumers, and so causing confusion with that person’s goods or business.

Unfair Competition Prevention Act, Article 2(1)(ii) (Japanese original)

二 自己の商品等表示として他人の著名な商品等表示と同一若しくは類似のものを使用し、又はその商品等表示を使用した商品を譲渡し、引き渡し、譲渡若しくは引渡しのために展示し、輸出し、輸入し、若しくは電気通信回線を通じて提供する行為

Summary, not a translation: using as one’s own indication something the same as or similar to another person’s famous indication of goods or business.

Unfair Competition Prevention Act, Article 2(1)(iii) (Japanese original)

三 他人の商品の形態（当該商品の機能を確保するために不可欠な形態を除く。）を模倣した商品を譲渡し、貸し渡し、譲渡若しくは貸渡しのために展示し、輸出し、輸入し、又は電気通信回線を通じて提供する行為

Summary, not a translation: transferring or offering goods that imitate the configuration of another person’s goods.

The logo is the initials of the operator’s name built from rectangles and polygons. The record of its making (the commits) shows no sign that it was based on anyone else’s indication.

No search has been made to compare it with other people’s indications or product configurations. The fact is that this has not been checked.

The typeface names in the previous section appear only as CSS names that call the reader’s own fonts. They are not used as an indication of this site.

Accessibility

The Act for Eliminating Discrimination against Persons with Disabilities (in Japanese, 障害を理由とする差別の解消の推進に関する法律) provides as follows. Only items 2 and 7 are cut out of Article 2.

Act for Eliminating Discrimination against Persons with Disabilities, Article 2(ii) (Japanese original)

二 社会的障壁 障害がある者にとって日常生活又は社会生活を営む上で障壁となるような社会における事物、制度、慣行、観念その他一切のものをいう。

Summary, not a translation: social barriers are things, systems, practices, ideas and anything else in society that act as barriers to daily or social life for persons with disabilities.

Act for Eliminating Discrimination against Persons with Disabilities, Article 2(vii) (Japanese original)

七 事業者 商業その他の事業を行う者（国、独立行政法人等、地方公共団体及び地方独立行政法人を除く。）をいう。

Summary, not a translation: a business operator is a person who carries on commerce or another business, other than the State, incorporated administrative agencies, local governments and local incorporated administrative agencies.

Act for Eliminating Discrimination against Persons with Disabilities, Article 8 (Japanese original)

事業者は、その事業を行うに当たり、障害を理由として障害者でない者と不当な差別的取扱いをすることにより、障害者の権利利益を侵害してはならない。 ２ 事業者は、その事業を行うに当たり、障害者から現に社会的障壁の除去を必要としている旨の意思の表明があった場合において、その実施に伴う負担が過重でないときは、障害者の権利利益を侵害することとならないよう、当該障害者の性別、年齢及び障害の状態に応じて、社会的障壁の除去の実施について必要かつ合理的な配慮をしなければならない。

Summary, not a translation: in carrying on its business, a business operator must not infringe the rights of persons with disabilities by unjust discrimination on the basis of disability, and, when a person with a disability expresses a need for a social barrier to be removed and the burden is not excessive, must make the necessary and reasonable accommodation.

Whether the operator is a “business operator” under this Act is not settled by the text of the Act. This page decides neither way.

With that said, what this site does is listed below, limited to what its checks verify.

  • The ratio of body text colours (--text, --muted, --faint, --accent) to the background is at least 4.5:1, in the light and the dark scheme. check_contrast.js measures it page by page. The same ratios are measured against the lower colour of surfaces (--card-lo) and against the background with the light (--glow) laid over it.
  • Shapes that are not text, such as the gold and ink of the band and the edge of a card against its ground, have a ratio of at least 3:1. The same check_contrast.js measures them.
  • Links in body text are shown by an underline as well as by colour, so that colour is not the only cue. check_contrast.js checks that the underline rule remains.
  • When the device asks for reduced motion (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce), the pages stop their motion. Section 10.69 of check_site.js checks that every generated page has that rule.
  • Surfaces get their height from the shadow variables. Parts that can be touched rise a little when pointed at or focused. With reduced motion they do not rise, and only the change of shadow remains. Section 10.70 of check_site.js checks that every generated page has that rule.
  • Images carry alternative text, and decorative SVG is hidden from screen readers. The figure SVG carries an aria-label that describes it. Sections 10.66, 10.68 and 10.69 of check_site.js check this.
  • That every page can be followed by keyboard alone, that the focus ring is visible and that the tab order follows the reading order, check_keyboard.js measures in a browser. Section 10.58 of check_site.js checks that no rule removes the focus ring and that the skip link has a target.
  • At widths of 1440, 1200, 900, 390 and 360 px, no page spills sideways, and none does with text at 200% at widths of 1280 and 1024 px. check_keyboard.js measures this.
  • Pages have dark-scheme colours that follow the device setting. Section 10.66 of check_site.js checks this.

The pages have never been read through with screen-reader software. The fact is that this has not been checked.

People and organisations

No image on this site shows the face or figure of a real person. The site rules (docs/site-rules.md) set that no portrait photograph is placed.

No image uses the logo, emblem or colours of a real organisation. Names of organisations appear on the pages only as words in the text.

The images served are only those listed in the table of parts. Section 10.69 of check_site.js checks that the image files served match that table exactly.

When something changes

When a part changes, this page and the checks are corrected in the same pull request.

Section 10.69 of check_site.js fails when the table of parts and the files served drift apart, when a typeface name changes, or when a quoted article differs from the copy.

When this page changes, the date in its footer changes. Earlier versions remain in the history of the public repository.
